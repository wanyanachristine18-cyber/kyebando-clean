03 / Business-Waste Rules

KCCA Guidelines & Fees for Shops, Clinics & Restaurants

Under the Kampala Capital City Authority Solid Waste Management Ordinance, commercial premises, retail shops, restaurants, salons, and clinics are classified as commercial waste generators and cannot rely on domestic household bag rates.

  • Mandatory private contract: Businesses must contract directly with a KCCA-licensed waste collector (such as Nabugabo Updeal JV or Bins Kampala Ltd) and keep valid payment receipts on site for inspection.
  • Standard commercial rates: Small shops typically pay Shs 15,000 – 30,000/month; busy restaurants, lodges, and clinics pay Shs 50,000 – 150,000/month depending on bin size and lift frequency.
  • On-site containment: Commercial waste must be kept inside covered bins or tied sacks within the business premises until collection—never placed on Gayaza Road pavements.
